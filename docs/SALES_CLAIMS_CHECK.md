## Copy-only fixes made

- Replaced the old GTM statement that over-cap enquiries were rejected with `DELIVER_ALL` and optional `LOCK_DETAILS` behavior.
- Updated the plan for consent-gated visitors/chat attribution and internal response metrics.
- Kept price ranges as hypotheses; no catalog values changed.
- Added explicit no-guarantee, sponsored-label, manual verification and no-auto-send wording.

## Decisions needed

1. Approve `LEAD_CAP_MODE`: default `DELIVER_ALL` or opt into `LOCK_DETAILS`; decide exactly which lead fields are unlocked by upgrade.
2. Confirm the lead-cap period and meaning: all enquiries, valid enquiries only, or another policy. Current count is all stored enquiries in the UTC calendar month.
3. Decide whether founding reminders and expiry emails may be enabled. They are currently blocked unless `FOUNDING_PERIOD_EMAILS_ENABLED=true`; enabling sends messages automatically.
4. Confirm Gold founding entitlement: current grant uses existing Gold plan terms and creates one `FEATURED` placement. Confirm whether this should be equivalent to Gold or adjusted, without changing catalog price until approved.
5. Approve internal response definition and the provisional slow-response thresholds; response time is not public.
6. Confirm local versus overseas placement categories, license registry and who verifies/rechecks licenses.
7. Confirm owner and timing for agency sales/support, refund/no-lead policy, commission policy, and how contact consent/source retention should work.
8. Confirm whether prospect records should have a fixed retention period and the minimum evidence needed for verified-request deletion.
9. Confirm production analytics/legal consent text before attributing UTM/referrer data; only analytics-consenting visitors are attributed.
