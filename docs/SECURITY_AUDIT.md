## Operational boundary

The `security@findmaids.ph` address in `.well-known/security.txt` is a placeholder until the owner verifies and staffs that mailbox. No production database, cloud account, DNS, payment provider, email provider, or real vendor webhook was accessed. No secrets or credentials were added. Security sign-off requires staging verification and an independent review of deployment settings.
