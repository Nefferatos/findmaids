## Start with your requirements

Write down the schedule, duties, location, experience, and languages that matter to your household. Discuss expectations clearly with any agency you contact.

## Check information directly

Use directory profiles to compare details, then independently confirm licences, fees, contract terms, and current availability with the agency.

## Ask practical questions

Ask how screening works, what support is provided after placement, how concerns are handled, and which costs may apply. Keep written copies of important terms.

FindMaids is a directory, not a recruiter, and does not guarantee agency services.
