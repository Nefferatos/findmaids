## Be specific about the role

Describe the work, daily routine, rest periods, household members, and any care needs. A clear conversation helps both sides decide whether the arrangement is suitable.

## Discuss terms openly

Confirm responsibilities, compensation, days off, accommodation if applicable, and the process for resolving problems. Ask the agency to explain any unclear terms before agreeing.

## Protect personal information

Only share information needed for the conversation, and ask how it will be used. Do not send identity documents through an unverified channel.
